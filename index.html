How the Danish series are classified

Denmark uses a closed 8-digit plan with no area codes. The first two digits decide the series. The table below follows the overall numbering plan as summarised on Wikipedia and published by the Danish authorities. Some fixed series carry block-level mobile exceptions and are marked mixed.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is looked up against carriers or messaging services, so the page can tell you which candidates are possible subscriber numbers, not which one belongs to a given person. Plan sources: Telephone numbers in Denmark, Danish numbering (digst.dk).
